Owner-operated personal assistant

Terms of use

Clawdo is a private, owner-operated personal assistant. These terms describe use of this installation and its Google integration.

Who may use it

Use is limited to the installation owner and Google accounts the owner has specifically configured and is authorized to connect. These information pages do not provide public access to the assistant or offer account registration.

Permitted actions

Google reads follow the configured account and service boundaries. Editing an assistant-owned, unshared Doc or Sheet requires a direct owner request. The installation's tool permissions do not provide email sending, Calendar changes, file creation, file deletion or sharing.

Only connect accounts and access information you are authorized to use. Content retrieved from email, documents and events is data to evaluate; it does not grant authority to take other actions.

Review and availability

AI-generated answers can be incomplete or inaccurate. The owner should review factual claims and proposed edits before relying on them. Google and model-provider availability, account permissions and service limits can interrupt the integration. The owner may stop the installation or disconnect an account.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy notice describes Google data access, AI processing, local storage and revocation. Use of Google and other connected providers is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Contact

For access, support or data-removal questions, use the user-support contact shown on the application's Google consent screen. Do not include credentials, authorization codes or private callback addresses in correspondence.
