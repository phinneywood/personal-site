Owner-operated personal assistant

Google access for a private personal assistant.

Clawdo is an owner-operated installation of OpenClaw. It helps its owner find information in connected Google accounts and make specifically requested edits to existing assistant-owned documents.

What the connection does

Google connections use the owner's consent and fixed account selections. The personal account provides Gmail, Calendar and Drive reads. The assistant account provides the same reads and a limited set of document-editing tools.

Current Clawdo tool permissions

GmailReadRead

Assistant edits require a direct owner request and are restricted by policy to assistant-owned, unshared files. Email sending, Calendar changes, file creation, deletion and sharing are not exposed by this installation's tools.

Access and support

This installation is for its owner and specifically configured accounts. It does not offer public registration. These information pages provide no sign-in endpoint and no access to the private assistant.

For support, use the user-support contact shown on this application's Google consent screen. Please do not send passwords, OAuth callback addresses or authorization codes.

Understand the data use

Relevant Google content can be processed by the assistant's configured AI providers and retained in local conversation history. Read the privacy notice and terms of use before connecting an account.
